What you're working toward

By the end of this activity, you'll be able to identify whether a historical source is primary or secondary, explain how you know, and compare your reasoning against sources other classmates examined.

How this activity works

  • Your Artifact
    ~10 min

    Examine your assigned source

    You'll be shown one historical artifact. Look at it closely, then answer four sourcing questions about who made it, when, whether you'd trust it, and whether it's primary or secondary.

  • Compare
    ~10 min

    Check two other artifacts

    Browse the other five artifacts in this set and pick two to look at — ideally one primary and one secondary. Fill in a short chart for each: who made it, when, whether you trust it, and its type.

  • Reflection
    ~5 min

    Reflect on what you noticed

    Answer two short reflection questions about what makes primary and secondary sources different.

01 Who wrote or created this source?

02 When was this document written or created?

03 Do you trust this document?

04 Is this a primary or secondary source?

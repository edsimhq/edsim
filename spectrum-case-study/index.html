What you're doing

Your group has been assigned one real society. You'll read a short description, select the sentences that answer five guiding questions, then decide where your society falls on the Freedom ↔ Order spectrum — and defend it with evidence.

About 20–25 minutes

The four tabs

  • Case Study — read the description, select evidence, answer 5 guiding questions
  • Placement — place your society on the 5-zone spectrum and justify it
  • Class Report — your one-minute report auto-builds from your best evidence
  • Export — save or copy your work when you're done

End goal

A one-minute class report: your society's name, its placement, and your single strongest piece of evidence. There's no right or wrong placement — this is a defensible judgment call. What matters is that your evidence actually supports where you put it.

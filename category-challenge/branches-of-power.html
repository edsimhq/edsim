Balancing the Branches

Separation of powers: Legislative, Executive, and Judicial

Welcome

The U.S. Constitution divides the power of the federal government among three branches so that no single branch becomes too powerful. In this activity, you'll learn what each branch does, test yourself on their powers, and then apply what you've learned to real historical and current events.

  • 1
    Meet the Branches Learn the role and key powers of the Legislative, Executive, and Judicial branches.
    About 5 minutes
  • 2
    Power Match Match each power to the branch that holds it. If it's tricky the first time, you'll get a shorter second round focused on the essentials.
    About 5–10 minutes
  • 3
    Scenario Check Read six real scenarios — some historical, some current — and figure out which branch and which power is at work in each one.
    About 10–15 minutes

By the end of this activity, you'll be able to identify which branch exercises a given power, and recognize separation of powers at work in real situations.

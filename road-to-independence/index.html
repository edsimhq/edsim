Road to Independence

EdSim · US History · Cause and Effect

Why did thirteen British colonies decide to break away? In this activity you will build the chain of events that led from the French and Indian War to the Declaration of Independence, and explain how each step led to the next. Tomorrow you will use this chain to test the Declaration’s complaints.

1

Round 1 · The War and the New Course (1754–1765)

Put the cards in cause-and-effect order to show how winning a war led Britain to tighten control. Then explain each link.

About 10 minutes

How to use the cards

  • Click or tap a card to pick it up, then click or tap a slot to place it. You can also drag and drop.
  • Click a placed card to pick it back up and move it.
  • Press Check any time. It flags problems but never gives away the answer.
  • Once two neighboring cards are placed, an explanation box appears. Finish the sentence starter in your own words. This is where your thinking shows.
  • Mode (top right): Supported gives you some cards, Regular is the standard activity, and Challenge hides the dates. Reading level changes the wording of the cards.
  • Your work saves on this device as you go.

By the end, you will have a complete cause-and-effect chain from 1754 to 1776 and a written explanation of how Britain’s actions and the colonists’ responses pushed the two sides toward war.

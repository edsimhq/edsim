Declaration Trace-Back

EdSim · US History · Cause and Effect

The Declaration of Independence lists complaints against King George III. Each one points back to something Britain did. In this activity you will trace six complaints back to the British actions you studied on Day 1, then decide which big idea from the Declaration’s opening each complaint breaks.

1

Trace-Back

Read each complaint. Choose the British action(s) behind it and the principle(s) it violates. Then explain your thinking in a sentence or two. There may be more than one good answer.

How This Works
Goal: Build your command of key vocabulary for the Civil War and Reconstruction — terms you need to recognize, define, and use on the Regents.

Modes: After setup, choose from three study modes — Review (learn definitions with cards), Practice (multiple-choice), or Blitz (timed challenge).

Time: About 10–15 minutes per session. You can stop and return anytime — your progress is saved on this device.

Setup: Choose your level and difficulty below, then hit Start studying.

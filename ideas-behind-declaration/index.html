Ideas Behind the Declaration

EdSim · US History · The Enlightenment

Before the Declaration of Independence complains about King George III, it explains the ideas that make those complaints matter. In this activity you will decode the Declaration’s opening, find where the ideas came from, and build the argument step by step.

1

Part 1 · Decode the Opening

Sort five lines from the Declaration into three big ideas. Then choose the best plain-English meaning for each line.

Natural rightsConsent of the governedSocial contract
About 8 minutes
2

Part 2 · Where Did These Ideas Come From?

Match four Enlightenment ideas to the thinker who wrote them. Then decide whether each idea shows up in the Declaration’s opening.

About 5 minutes
3

Part 3 · The Declaration’s Argument

Put the steps of the Declaration’s argument in order, from natural rights to independence. Then explain each link.

About 8 minutes

How to use this activity

  • Click or tap a card to pick it up, then click or tap a spot to place it. You can also drag and drop. Click a placed card to pick it back up.
  • Press Check any time. It flags problems but never gives away the answer.
  • Underlined words have tooltips. Hover or focus on them for a definition.
  • Mode (top right): Supported gives you a head start. Reading level changes the wording of the explanations.
  • Your work saves on this device as you go.

WHAT IS SURVEY SHOWDOWN?
--------------------------
Survey Showdown is a Family Feud-style classroom game built on
real New York Regents US History content. Students compete to
name the most popular answers to survey questions — "100
historians were polled..." — with answers ranked by how often
they appeared in Regents exam responses.

There are two modes: Class Mode (teacher-led, whole class) and
Solo Mode (individual, self-paced).


================================================================
CLASS MODE — HOW TO RUN IT
================================================================

CLASS MODE requires two browser windows open on the SAME
computer. One window is your teacher dashboard (host view).
The other is the student scoreboard, projected for the class.

STEP 1 — Open the Teacher Dashboard
  - From the home page, click "Teacher / Host View"
  - A new window opens. Enter the password: feud123
  - Select a unit from the dropdown
  - Enter team names (or leave as defaults)
  - Click "Open Student Scoreboard" — this opens a second window
  - Drag that second window to your projector or smartboard

STEP 2 — Start the Game
  - Click "Start Game" in the teacher dashboard
  - The student scoreboard will activate automatically
  - The question is displayed on the scoreboard
  - Answers are hidden — only you can see them

STEP 3 — Run the Round
  - Call on students to guess answers
  - Click REVEAL next to each correct answer in your dashboard
  - The answer appears on the projected scoreboard in real time
  - Click the strike dots (✕) when a team gets one wrong
  - After 3 strikes, offer the steal to the opposing team

STEP 4 — Scoring
  - Click STEAL ✓ or MISSED ✗ to resolve steal attempts
  - Award the round pot to the winning team using the turn buttons
  - Click "Next Round →" to advance

STEP 5 — Word Bank (Optional Scaffold)
  - Toggle "Show word bank" in your dashboard at any time
  - A word bank appears on the student scoreboard with real
    answers mixed with phantom distractors from other rounds
  - Use the difficulty slider to control how many phantoms appear:
      Fewer phantoms = easier (less noise, more scaffold)
      More phantoms = harder (real answers buried deeper)
  - Toggle it on or off mid-round — great for differentiation


================================================================
SOLO MODE — HOW TO RUN IT
================================================================

Solo Mode is self-paced and requires no teacher involvement
once students load it.

  - Students select a unit from the dropdown
  - Enter their name (optional)
  - Toggle AI feedback on or off
  - Click Start Game

GAMEPLAY:
  - 10 answer chips appear — 5 real answers, 5 distractors
  - Click a chip to guess. Correct = snaps into its slot.
  - Wrong = strike. 3 strikes = steal opportunity.
  - AI feedback (when on) explains why a wrong answer doesn't
    fit — powered by Claude (Anthropic).

NOTE: AI feedback requires an internet connection. It fails
silently if unavailable — students just see the chip bounce
back. The rest of the game works offline.

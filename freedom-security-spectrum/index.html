Freedom vs. Security
A Spectrum of Government Control

🎯 What You're Working Toward

Every government makes a trade-off: the more control it has, the more order and safety it can provide — but the less freedom its citizens keep. By the end of this activity, you'll be able to explain that trade-off using real examples, and you'll be ready to place real societies on this same spectrum in a follow-up activity.

🧭 What's In This Activity

  • Explore the Spectrum — Drag a slider from Anarchy to Totalitarianism and watch how four rights (what you can do, what you can say, who makes the rules, and how much safety the government provides) change along the way.
  • Checkpoint — A quick drag-and-drop sort to check your understanding before you move on.
  • Track a Right — Once you've completed the checkpoint, you'll unlock a focused mode where you follow just one right across the whole spectrum.

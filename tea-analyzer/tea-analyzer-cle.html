Civic Literacy Essay
Your goal: Practice the TEA method — Topic, Evidence, Analysis — using the primary source documents from this exam. By the end, you will have a complete set of notes you can use to write the Civic Literacy Essay.
What TEA means
T
Topic — The question you need to answer. It's already filled in for you — you just need to read it carefully before you look at the document.
E
Evidence — The specific sentence or detail from the document that best answers the question. Click the sentence in the document that you think is the best answer. You'll get instant feedback.
A
Analysis — Your explanation of why the evidence matters. Good analysis sentences start with "This shows..." or "This means..." You can use a sentence starter, try the multiple choice, or write your own.
Documents
One document per tab
Each tab has a document and three steps: Topic (auto-filled), Evidence (click to select), and Analysis (write or scaffold). Spend about 5–7 minutes per document.
Analysis Scaffolds
Two ways to get help
Stuck on analysis? Click Sentence Starters to pick a starter you finish yourself, or Multiple Choice to choose the best analysis sentence. Or turn both off and write your own.
